Our Story

Chicken is the star — crispy or grilled, fresh to order, never frozen

Fast casual and quality were never meant to be a compromise

A Note From Our Founder, John Kunkel

Chicken is the star — crispy or grilled, fresh to order, never frozen. We're always striving for sustainability by sourcing local ingredients as often as possible. Our birds are 100% natural, with no steroids, hormones, or antibiotics, and Spring Chicken's packaging is produced to be lean and green with eco-friendly, recyclable materials.

Growing With Intention

The Team
Behind It

Behind Spring Chicken is a team of operators who've spent decades building and scaling some of the country's most respected hospitality brands. Here are some of the other concepts the team has built.

Yardbird

The celebrated Southern-inspired chicken concept that started it all — one of the most respected fried chicken kitchens in the country, and the roots Spring Chicken was built from.

Wakuda

A vibrant celebration of Japanese-Peruvian Nikkei cuisine, pairing bold, chef-driven flavors with a striking, larger-than-life dining room.

Chica

Latin-inspired small plates and craft cocktails from Chef Lorena Garcia, built for sharing and built for a scene.
